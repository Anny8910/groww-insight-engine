--- FEE EXPLAINER (reusable support snippet) ---
Exit load is a fee charged when redeeming units of a mutual fund before the minimum holding period specified by the fund house.
The fee is calculated on the value of the units redeemed and is deducted automatically at the time of redemption.
The holding period is counted from the date of each individual SIP installment, so a partial period can trigger the exit load even if the overall investment is longer.
Sources: Groww Help — What are the charges applicable for redeeming? (https://groww.in/help/mutual-funds/order/what-are-the-charges-applicable-for-redeeming--53) | SEBI Investor — Exit Load (https://investor.sebi.gov.in/exit_load.html)
